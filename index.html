How to play

  1. Read the two sentences.
  2. Drag a conjunction piece from the bottom into the gap over the river.
  3. Right piece: the bridge is built and you see what the word does. Wrong piece: the bridge falls!
  4. Some questions have more than one answer. Find them all, then press Next.
